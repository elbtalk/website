English Levels

Choose your learning path

A1 Beginner

Start with everyday English.

  • Skills: basic questions
  • Vocabulary: family, work, places
  • Grammar: verb to be, present simple
  • Speaking: short answers

A2 Elementary

Build confidence in daily situations.

  • Skills: routines and descriptions
  • Vocabulary: food, travel, shopping
  • Grammar: past simple, comparatives
  • Speaking: simple conversations

B1 Intermediate

Express ideas and experiences clearly.

  • Skills: opinions and storytelling
  • Vocabulary: work, culture, technology
  • Grammar: present perfect, modals
  • Speaking: longer answers

B2 Upper Intermediate

Communicate naturally and professionally.

  • Skills: debates and presentations
  • Vocabulary: business and current events
  • Grammar: passive, reported speech
  • Speaking: fluency and interaction

C1 Advanced

Refine accuracy, nuance and impact.

  • Skills: persuasion and leadership
  • Vocabulary: idioms and collocations
  • Grammar: inversion and emphasis
  • Speaking: confident communication
